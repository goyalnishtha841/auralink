Questions people ask

Why does the passenger screen ask for a language?

Each passenger picks their own language the first time they open their seat (English, Hindi, Tamil or Thai, each also written in English). The phone remembers it. If the passenger chose a language at check-in, the seat opens in it straight away. Crew always see Japanese and English.

What is the crew PIN?

A 6-digit code, like a door code. Passengers and crew share the same Wi-Fi, so the crew screens are locked. It is shown above (only on this laptop) and printed in the terminal when Auralink starts. Each device asks once, then stays unlocked for 24 hours.

Does it need the internet?

No. Speech to text, matching, fonts and every screen run on this laptop. Phones only need to reach the laptop over Wi-Fi. You can unplug the internet during the demo to prove it.

A phone cannot open the QR link

The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this laptop, and mobile data should be off or it may try the internet. Some guest Wi-Fi networks block devices from seeing each other: use a phone hotspot or your own router.
